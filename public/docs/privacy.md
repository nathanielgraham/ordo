# Ordo Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 13, 2026

### Information We Collect
- Account information (email, organization details, user ID)
- Usage data (API calls, job metadata, execution logs)
- Server monitoring data (CPU, memory, disk, etc.) when you enable monitoring
- Technical data (IP addresses, connection metadata)

### How We Use Your Information
- To provide, maintain, and improve the Ordo scheduling service
- To enforce usage limits and billing
- To send important service notifications
- To analyze usage patterns

### Data Sharing
We do **not** sell your personal data. Limited sharing occurs only with service providers under strict confidentiality agreements.

### Self-Hosted Deployments
For self-hosted or air-gapped installations, all data remains under your full control. We do not collect usage data unless you explicitly enable telemetry.

### Data Retention & Deletion
We retain usage data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing ordosched@proton.me.

### Security
We use industry-standard practices, but no system is 100% secure.

### Your Rights
You may access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting us.

### Governing Law
This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota, USA.
