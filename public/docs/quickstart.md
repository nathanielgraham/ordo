# Quickstart

Welcome to Ordo! Let's get your first workflow running in under 5 minutes.

## 1. Login
- Open Ordo in your browser
- Sign in with your credentials

## 2. Create Your First Cluster
1. Go to **Jobs & Clusters**
2. Click the ellipsis on "root/" → **Run** (or create a new cluster)

## 3. Add a Job
- In a cluster, click ellipsis → add job with a simple script like `echo "Hello Ordo!"`

## 4. Run It
- Click **Run** on the job
- View the log with **View Log**

Congratulations! You just ran your first job.
